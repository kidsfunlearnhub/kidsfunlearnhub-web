About The Find The Vehicle Activity

Welcome to KidsFunLearnHub's Find The Vehicle! This free educational activity builds children's vocabulary and recognition skills by having them listen to audio prompts and select the correct vehicle.

Learning Outcomes: Auditory processing, visual recognition, and hand-eye coordination. Available in English, Hindi, and Marathi!
