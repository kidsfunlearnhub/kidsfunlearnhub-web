About The Varnmala Shadow Match Activity

Welcome to KidsFunLearnHub's Varnmala Shadow Match! This free educational puzzle helps children develop visual discrimination and fine motor skills by connecting Hindi and Marathi Devanagari characters to their silhouettes.

Learning Outcomes: Character recognition, logic, phonetic vocabulary, and hand-eye coordination. Play in a fun, ad-free environment!
