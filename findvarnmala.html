About The Find Varnmala Activity

Welcome to KidsFunLearnHub's Find Varnmala! This free educational activity is designed specifically for toddlers, preschoolers, and early learners. By listening to the audio prompts and selecting the correct Devanagari letter from the grid, children actively build their phonetic awareness and letter recognition skills in Hindi and Marathi.

Learning Outcomes: Auditory processing, visual recognition, early reading skills, and hand-eye coordination. Play in a fun, ad-free environment!
