About The Varnmala Puzzle Match Activity

Welcome to KidsFunLearnHub's Varnmala Puzzle Match! This interactive drag-and-drop activity helps preschoolers build vocabulary and fine motor skills by piecing together Hindi and Marathi letters with their matching objects.

Learning Outcomes: Letter-object association, visual logic, phonetic vocabulary, and touchscreen coordination. Play in a fun, ad-free environment!
