About The Find The Body Part Game

Welcome to the Find The Body Part Game! This fundamental educational tool helps young children build self-awareness by asking them to identify specific anatomical features, like a hand or eye, from a lineup of four options.

Learning Outcomes: Physical self-awareness, personal autonomy vocabulary, visual discrimination, and multi-language learning.

How to Play & Educational Benefits

Learning body parts is a critical milestone for personal autonomy, allowing toddlers to communicate their physical needs clearly. This multiple-choice format makes it easy for children to bridge digital learning with physical awareness, as they often touch their own nose or ear while finding the corresponding image on the screen.

Physical vocabulary is highly important to localize. That is why this game features comprehensive English, Hindi, and Marathi support. A child will learn that "Head" translates to 'Sir' (सिर) in Hindi and 'Doke' (डोके) in Marathi, giving them the exact words they need at home.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is this finding game suitable for 2-year-olds?
Yes! Pointing to body parts on command is a standard 18-to-24-month developmental milestone.

Do you offer offline activities for body parts?
We offer corresponding printable worksheets in the Parents Corner to reinforce this vocabulary away from the screen.
