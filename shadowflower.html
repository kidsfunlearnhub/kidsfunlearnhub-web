About The Flower Shadow Match Activity

Welcome to KidsFunLearnHub's Flower Shadow Match! This free educational puzzle helps children develop visual discrimination and fine motor skills by connecting beautiful flowers and plants to their silhouettes.

Learning Outcomes: Shape recognition, logic, nature vocabulary, and hand-eye coordination. Available in English, Hindi, and Marathi!
