About The Number Shadow Match Activity

Welcome to KidsFunLearnHub's Number Shadow Match! This free educational puzzle helps children develop visual discrimination and fine motor skills by connecting numbers (1-40) to their silhouettes in English, Hindi, and Marathi.

Learning Outcomes: Number recognition, logic, math vocabulary, and hand-eye coordination. Play in a fun, ad-free environment!
