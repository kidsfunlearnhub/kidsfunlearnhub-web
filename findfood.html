About The Find The Food Activity

Welcome to KidsFunLearnHub's Find The Food! This free educational activity builds children's vocabulary and recognition skills by having them listen to audio prompts and select the correct Indian food or dish.

Learning Outcomes: Auditory processing, visual recognition, and cultural food vocabulary. Available in English, Hindi, and Marathi!
