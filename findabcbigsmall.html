About The Find Big & Small Activity

Welcome to KidsFunLearnHub's Find Big & Small Letters! This free educational activity is designed specifically for toddlers, preschoolers, and early learners. By listening to the audio prompts and visually matching the Capital letter to its lowercase buddy, children actively build their phonetic awareness and letter recognition skills.

Learning Outcomes: Auditory processing, visual recognition, early reading skills, and uppercase/lowercase association. Fully voiced and available in English, Hindi, and Marathi!
