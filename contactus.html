Frequently Asked Questions:

How can I request new worksheets in Hindi or Marathi?

You can use the contact form below to request specific topics. We are constantly expanding our Parents Corner with new printable PDFs for offline practice to support regional linguistic formats.

Are the interactive games completely free to use?

Yes! All our digital activities across the Activity Hub, including shadow matching, tracing, and finding games, are 100% free for toddlers and preschoolers.

Is there a KidsFunLearnHub mobile app available?

Currently, our platform is fully web-based and optimized to run smoothly on all mobile browsers and tablets. However, we are actively planning to launch a dedicated cross-platform mobile application in the future to make offline learning even more accessible.
