Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Our Commitment to Children’s Privacy

KidsFunLearnHub is designed especially for children and families. We respect the privacy of children, strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and are committed to providing a safe learning environment. We do not require visitors to create an account, thereby ensuring an anonymous and secure browsing experience for all toddlers and preschoolers.

2. Information We Collect

Our website does not require children to provide personal information to use learning features. We do not knowingly collect personal data such as names, addresses, or contact details from children. Like most standard websites, our hosting servers may automatically log non-personally identifiable information such as browser type, IP address, device type, and the date/time of visits to help us administer the site and analyze broad user trends.

3. Optional Parent Communication & Data Retention

If parents choose to contact us for feedback or support (support@kidsfunlearnhub.com), they may voluntarily provide an email address. This information is used only to respond to inquiries and is never sold or shared. Any emails or communication records are routinely deleted after the inquiry has been fully addressed to ensure minimal data retention.

4. Third-Party Links & Affiliate Disclosure

Our website may contain links to external resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites. Parents are encouraged to review those privacy policies. Additionally, KidsFunLearnHub may participate in affiliate marketing programs, such as Flipkart and EarnKaro. This means we may earn a small commission if parents click on certain external links and make a purchase, at no additional cost to you.

5. Advertising

We use Google AdSense to fund our free platform. We have configured our AdSense account to tag site requests for 'Child-Directed Treatment' to ensure ads are safe and non-personalized (no behavioral tracking) for young users. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to other websites. Parents may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

6. Cookies & Basic Analytics

We may use minimal cookies or anonymous analytics to improve website performance, monitor offline PDF downloads, and enhance the language learning experience. No personal identity is tracked. You can choose to disable or selectively turn off our cookies or third-party cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect how you interact with our interactive dashboard.

7. Security & Governing Law

We take reasonable technical and administrative steps to protect all information and maintain a child-friendly, secure platform. Our privacy practices and website operations are governed by the laws of India. Any disputes arising from the use of this website shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in Maharashtra, India.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to improve clarity, reflect new features, or comply with evolving legal requirements. Any structural changes will be posted immediately on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries, parental requests regarding data, or general concerns, parents may email us directly at support@kidsfunlearnhub.com or use our Contact Page.

Our goal is simple: safe, joyful, and educational learning for children.
