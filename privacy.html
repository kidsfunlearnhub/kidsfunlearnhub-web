Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Our Commitment to Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

KidsFunLearnHub (our website and mobile application, collectively the 'Platform') is designed especially for children and families. We respect the privacy of children, strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and are committed to providing a safe learning environment. We do not require visitors to create an account, thereby ensuring an anonymous and secure browsing experience for all toddlers and preschoolers.

2. Information We Collect & Mobile Permissions

Our Platform does not require children to provide personal information to use learning features. We do not knowingly collect personal data such as names, addresses, or contact details from children. Like most standard websites, our hosting servers may automatically log non-personally identifiable information such as browser type, IP address, device type, and the date/time of visits. Mobile App Permissions: Our mobile application does not request, access, or collect sensitive device permissions such as the Camera, Microphone, precise Location (GPS), or Contacts. The app utilizes basic network permissions to access the internet and requests Notification access solely to alert you when new educational worksheets or content are added. Parents can manage or disable these notifications at any time through their device settings.

3. Optional Parent Communication & Data Retention

If parents choose to contact us for feedback or support (support@kidsfunlearnhub.com), they may voluntarily provide an email address. This information is used only to respond to inquiries and is never sold or shared. Any emails or communication records are routinely deleted after the inquiry has been fully addressed to ensure minimal data retention.

4. Third-Party Links, Affiliates, & Parental Gates

Our platform may contain links to external resources or participate in affiliate marketing programs (such as Flipkart and EarnKaro), where we may earn a small commission if parents make a purchase. To protect young users, any external or affiliate links within our mobile application are strictly secured behind a Parental Gate. This ensures that children cannot navigate away from the app unsupervised. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites and encourage parents to review them.

5. Advertising

The Kids Fun Learn Hub platform (both website and mobile application) is currently 100% ad-free. We do not display third-party advertisements or use personalized behavioral tracking on our young users.

6. Cookies & Local Device Storage

On our website, we may use minimal cookies or anonymous analytics to improve website performance. Within our mobile application, we utilize local on-device storage to save the child's learning progress or preferences. This data remains strictly on your personal device, is never transmitted to our servers, and can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's cache in your device settings.

7. Data Deletion Policy

Because our mobile application and website do not require account creation and do not collect personal identifying data, there is no personal user data stored on our servers to delete. Any locally stored app progress can be deleted by the parent by clearing the app data on their device. If a parent has emailed us for support, they may request the deletion of that email correspondence by contacting support@kidsfunlearnhub.com.

8. Security & Governing Law

We take reasonable technical and administrative steps to protect all information and maintain a child-friendly, secure platform. Our privacy practices and website operations are governed by the laws of India. Any disputes arising from the use of this website shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in Maharashtra, India.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to improve clarity, reflect new features, or comply with evolving legal requirements. Any structural changes will be posted immediately on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries, parental requests regarding data, or general concerns, parents may email us directly at support@kidsfunlearnhub.com or use our Contact Page.

Our goal is simple: safe, joyful, and educational learning for children.
