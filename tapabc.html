About The Tap The Alphabet Activity

Welcome to KidsFunLearnHub's Tap The Alphabet! This free educational activity is designed specifically for toddlers, preschoolers, and early learners. By finding and tapping all the correct letters scattered across the screen, children actively build their visual scanning, letter recognition, and hand-eye coordination skills.

Learning Outcomes: Visual tracking, cognitive speed, early reading skills, and phonetic awareness. Play in a fun, ad-free environment!
