About The Find The Flower Game

Welcome to the Find The Flower Game! This visually stimulating multiple-choice puzzle teaches children to identify various beautiful blooms by scanning four different floral options and picking the correct match.

Learning Outcomes: Nature appreciation, color gradient recognition, auditory processing, and sensory vocabulary.

How to Play & Educational Benefits

Distinguishing one flower from another is a fantastic exercise in noticing fine details. Finding a rose among a daisy, lotus, and sunflower requires the child to process differences in petal shape, layering, and color. This sharpens their deductive logic and observational skills.

The vibrant colors of this game provide a rich visual experience. Paired with our trilingual audio system, a child can successfully identify a lotus and learn its English name while simultaneously absorbing Hindi and Marathi translations like 'Kamal' (कमळ), making nature walks much more engaging.
