About The Number Quantity Puzzle

Welcome to KidsFunLearnHub's Number Puzzle Match! This interactive drag-and-drop activity helps preschoolers build early math skills by counting groups of objects and matching them to the exact numerical digit (1 through 20).

Learning Outcomes: Number-quantity association, visual counting, math vocabulary, and fine motor skills. Play in English, Hindi, or Marathi!
