About The Tap Varnmala Activity

Welcome to KidsFunLearnHub's Tap Varnmala! This free educational activity is designed specifically to help toddlers and preschoolers recognize Hindi and Marathi Devanagari characters. By finding and tapping all the correct letters scattered across the screen, children actively build their phonetic foundations, visual scanning, and hand-eye coordination skills.

Learning Outcomes: Character recognition, cognitive speed, early reading skills, and visual tracking. Play in a fun, ad-free environment!
