About The Big & Small Puzzle Match Activity

Welcome to KidsFunLearnHub's Big & Small Letter Puzzle! This interactive drag-and-drop activity helps children develop visual discrimination and early reading skills by physically connecting Capital letters to their lowercase partners.

Learning Outcomes: Uppercase/lowercase association, logic, fine motor skills, and touch-screen coordination. Play in a fun, ad-free environment!
