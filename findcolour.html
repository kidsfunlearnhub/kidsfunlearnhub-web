About The Find The Colour Activity

Welcome to KidsFunLearnHub's Find The Colour! This free educational activity builds children's vocabulary and recognition skills by having them listen to audio prompts and select the correct color.

Learning Outcomes: Auditory processing, visual recognition, and hand-eye coordination. Available in English, Hindi, and Marathi!
